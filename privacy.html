Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Black Zoo Studio is a private tool operated by Ray Santos for his own accounts only.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission (Google, TikTok or Instagram sign-in), it uploads videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails that the owner created, and reads basic channel information needed to do so.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the macOS Keychain. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Nothing is collected from anyone else.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account permissions, or in TikTok and Instagram app settings.
